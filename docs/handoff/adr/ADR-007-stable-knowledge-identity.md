# ADR 007 Stable Knowledge Identity

Status: Accepted

Decision: Knowledge IDs are opaque and stable across wording, prompt, and model changes. Type-specific structural signals drive identity. Merge is conservative, Phase 1 split requires review, and disappearance is handled through support health and delayed deprecation.

Consequences: semantic/presentation fingerprints are separate; identity evaluation fixtures are mandatory; human labels guide rather than lock facts forever.
